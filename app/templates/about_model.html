About the PitchIQ Model

PitchIQ is designed as a transparent baseball analytics project. It compares probable starting pitchers using season performance, recent form, Statcast-style quality indicators, command and whiff skill, lineup context, ballpark factors, and weather.

PitchIQ is a portfolio analytics project, not gambling advice. The model is pitcher-focused and does not fully account for bullpen, defense, injuries, betting markets, or complete offensive projections.

Formula & Stat Glossary

Pitcher Advantage Which starter has the edge?

The pitcher/team with the stronger PitchIQ matchup score. It is based on the score gap between the two probable starters.

PitchIQ Overall pitcher matchup score

A 20-80 style score blending season performance, recent form, Statcast quality, command/whiff skill, lineup matchup, weather, and park context. Higher is better for the pitcher.

Win Probability Pitcher-focused probability

Calculated from the PitchIQ score gap. This is conservative and pitcher-focused, not a full sportsbook-style win model.

Expected IP Projected starter workload

Starts with recent average innings, then adjusts modestly for pitch count, command, recent form, weather, and park context.

K Projection Projected strikeouts

Estimated batters faced multiplied by adjusted strikeout rate. Recent strikeout form is only a small nudge so one hot stretch does not dominate the projection.

Quality Start Chance of 6+ IP and ≤3 ER

Estimated from expected innings, season run prevention, recent form, Statcast quality, command, weather, and park context.

Model Role Away SP / Home SP

Shows whether the pitcher is being evaluated as the away starting pitcher or home starting pitcher.

Season Performance Season-level quality

Component score using ERA, WHIP, FIP, and strikeout skill.

Recent Form Recent-start trend

Component score using recent ERA, WHIP, strikeouts, average innings, pitch count, and velocity trend.

Statcast Quality Contact quality allowed

Component score using xwOBA, xBA, xSLG, hard-hit rate, barrel rate, and average exit velocity.

Command / Whiff Dominance and control

Component score using K%, BB%, chase rate, and whiff rate.

Lineup Matchup Opponent lineup context

Pitcher-facing score for the opposing lineup. When official lineups are not available, the app uses a neutral/deterministic placeholder.

Weather Impact Pitcher-facing weather score

Considers temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, and HR-prone profiles. Pitcher-friendly conditions score higher.

Park Context Ballpark run environment

Pitcher-facing score based on run factor and home-run factor. Pitcher-friendly parks score higher.

ERA Earned Run Average

Earned runs allowed per nine innings. Lower is better for pitchers.

WHIP Walks + hits per inning

Measures baserunners allowed. Lower is better.

FIP Fielding Independent Pitching

Estimates pitcher run prevention from strikeouts, walks/HBP, and home runs while reducing defensive noise.

K/9 Strikeouts per nine innings

Useful strikeout skill stat, but less direct than K% for projecting Ks because it depends on innings.

BB/9 Walks per nine innings

Lower is better.

HR/9 Home runs per nine innings

Lower is better, especially in hitter-friendly parks.

Last 3 ERA Recent ERA

ERA over the recent-start sample.

Last 3 WHIP Recent WHIP

WHIP over the recent-start sample.

Last 3 K Recent strikeouts

Total strikeouts over the recent-start sample. Used lightly in the model.

Avg IP Average innings

Average innings per start in the recent-start sample.

Avg Pitch Count Recent workload

Average recent pitch count. Higher pitch counts can support more expected innings.

Velocity Trend Recent velocity movement

Estimated recent velocity movement compared with baseline. Positive is generally better.

xwOBA Expected weighted on-base allowed

Expected weighted on-base average allowed based on contact quality and plate appearance outcomes. Lower is better.

xBA Expected batting average allowed

Lower is better for pitchers.

xSLG Expected slugging allowed

Lower is better for pitchers.

HardHit% Hard-hit rate

Percentage of batted balls hit 95+ mph. Lower is better.

Barrel% Barrel rate

Percentage of batted balls with ideal exit velocity and launch angle. Lower is better.

Avg EV Average exit velocity allowed

Lower is better for pitchers.

GB% Ground-ball percentage

More ground balls can reduce extra-base damage.

FB% Fly-ball percentage

Context dependent. High fly-ball rates can be risky in homer-friendly parks.

LD% Line-drive percentage

Lower is usually better for pitchers.

Pull% Pulled-contact percentage

High pull contact can indicate damage risk depending on hitter and park.

Oppo% Opposite-field contact percentage

Share of contact hit to the opposite field.

Zone% Zone rate

Percentage of pitches thrown in the strike zone.

Chase% Out-of-zone chase rate

Percentage of out-of-zone pitches that batters swing at. Higher is better for pitchers.

Whiff% Swing-and-miss rate

Higher usually supports stronger strikeout projection.

1stPitchS% First-pitch strike rate

Getting ahead early generally improves pitcher outcomes.

BB% Walk rate

Walks per plate appearance. Lower is better.

K% Strikeout rate

Strikeouts per plate appearance. One of the best anchors for strikeout projections because it is tied to batters faced.
